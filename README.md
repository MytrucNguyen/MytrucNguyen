## Hello World!

I'm Mytruc, a Full-Stack Software Engineer with 5 years of experience building enterprise-grade applications in React, TypeScript, Next.js, and Python. I lead features end-to-end, from product research and design through production.

My work includes building an analytics dashboard serving 1,200+ convenience stores, used daily by executives, designing internal operations platforms, architecting reusable component libraries with shadcn/Radix and Storybook, and owning website migrations across React, Astro, and Next.js. I also design REST APIs, write end-to-end tests with Playwright, and define engineering standards adopted by my teams.

I collaborate closely with product leads and designers, translating business requirements into technical decisions and owning the architecture behind them.
